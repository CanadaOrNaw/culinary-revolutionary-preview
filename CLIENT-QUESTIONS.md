## 3. Should answer — these directly cost bookings

**Pricing.** The site gives no number at all. A floor is enough — "private dinners from
$X per guest, minimum Y guests." Without it, people who were ready to pay can't tell if
they're in the right place, and the ones who inquire anyway vanish at the quote.

**Food-safety certification.** ServSafe? Florida food handler? Currently not mentioned.

**Liability insurance.** Carried? Can a certificate be issued to a venue? Corporate and
wedding clients often cannot book a vendor without one — this silently disqualifies the
highest-value work.

**Minimum guest count**, and whether there's a minimum spend.
